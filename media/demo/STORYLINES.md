# Relay launch film: three storylines

The one thing: when one Claude account runs out, Relay moves you to the one with room, from the menu bar.
The viewer remembers: "my limits stop being a wall; Relay knows which account has room and switches."
Facts come from README.md and the real preview UI; account names and figures are the preview's English
sample data.

**Picked: A**, because a stranger follows one person's afternoon and one real click from the problem to the
fix, and every frame is the real app.

## A · "The 4:47 PM limit" (picked, built)

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take.
The clock turns 4:47 PM and Personal · Max reaches Full in the real menu-bar panel; the cursor clicks
Switch on the suggested account and Relay's own switch window plays (the red panda walks through the door
and comes back in disguise: "You are on Personal · Backup"). The camera carries on into the main window
(the new account In use, every account's limits), Planning (5-hour windows draw across the day) and
Proxy & Gateway (turned on, requests going to the account with room), then the real app icon lands.

- First two seconds: the real panel at 2× on cream, "4:46 PM" beside it; the minute rolls to 4:47 and the
  5-hour row turns from 97 % to "Full · 1 h 50 min".
- Beats: hook (4:47, Full) → click Switch → switch window → Overview (In use, every limit) → Planning →
  Gateway → end card.
- Look: warm cream #f5f4f2, the real UI at 1.6–2× with its real labels, Inter titles in warm ink, Relay
  orange only on the click, the payoff and the end card.
- Sound: no music source available this run (no HeyGen sign-in); delivered silent, edit reads muted.

## B · "All at once"

Device: type carries the argument. Form: split screen to one.
Four account cards fill at different speeds in four panes while giant kinetic words count the waste;
the panes collapse into the one Overview board where every limit sits in one list.

- First two seconds: four panes, four real limit bars racing; one hits Full and flashes its own red.
- Beats: four panes filling → "one is always full" → collapse into the Overview → switch → planning grid
  → end card.
- Look: dark graphite (Relay's dark theme #100f0e / #eeebe7), giant type, one orange accent per frame.
- Sound: a tight pulse that cuts on the bar; collapse on a downbeat.

## C · "A day in 5-hour windows"

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: a flight through one world.
A 24-hour ruler is the set; the camera flies along it as each account's 5-hour window lights up and
hands off to the next before the current one runs out, then pulls back until the ruler is the Planning
screen's own Today chart.

- First two seconds: 09:00 on a ruler that runs off both edges of the frame; one window starts to fill.
- Beats: morning window → hand-off → lunch → hand-off → evening → pull back to Planning → end card.
- Look: cream ground, the Planning chart's own colours, a tick on every hour.
- Sound: clock ticks under a soft bed; a chime-free hand-off accent on each window.
